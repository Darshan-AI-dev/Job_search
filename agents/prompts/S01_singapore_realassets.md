## Your lane
Singapore is Asia's REIT capital and the region's real-asset headquarters. S-REIT managers,
sovereign investors and pan-Asian real estate platforms all run portfolio strategy, asset
management and capital planning functions here — the exact stack the candidate operates.

**Read the visa section of the geography brief before you score anything.** Singapore is not a
low-friction market for a mid-career foreign hire: financial-services Employment Pass requires
SGD 6,200/month minimum (rising to SGD 11,800 at 45+) plus 40 points on COMPASS, and the
financial-services floor rises again in January 2027. **Score each row one point below an
identical Gulf role** unless the employer is a large institution with a known sponsorship track
record, and put the sponsorship question explicitly in `Application_Notes`.
