## Your lane
The **less-indexed end of the Mumbai market**: private credit and special situations funds, family
offices and promoter investment offices, single-asset and club-deal platforms, and — importantly —
roles that only surface through **executive search and specialist recruiters** rather than public
job boards. Many of the best-fitting senior seats never reach Naukri, so this lane is partly about
mapping the *intermediaries* worth contacting, not only the postings.

## Special instruction for this lane
For every **recruiter / search firm** row: put the recruiter in `Company`, set `Company_Type` to
`Recruiter / Search Firm`, set `Listing_Status` honestly (usually `Target employer - not verified`),
and use `Application_Notes` to record **who to approach and with what angle** — this tab doubles as
the candidate's outreach map. A row that says "contact this firm's financial-services practice, lead
with fund-waterfall and asset-strategy experience" is more valuable here than a stale posting.

## Scoring note for this lane
Real estate private credit and real-asset special situations are 8–9. Generalist private credit is
7. Family-office generalist investment roles are 6–7 (broad mandate, small team, high variance —
note that). Recruiter-mapping rows should be scored on the *quality of the channel*, typically 5–7,
with the reasoning stated in `Fit_Rationale`.
