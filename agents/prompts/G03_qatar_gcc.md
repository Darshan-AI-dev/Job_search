## Your lane
Qatar first — a smaller, more state-linked market than the UAE, but one with deep sovereign
capital and a real estate sector still professionalising its asset management. Then a secondary
sweep of Saudi Arabia, where Vision 2030 giga-projects and PIF's portfolio companies are hiring
real-estate and fund talent aggressively and pay well above Gulf norms to attract it.

Be candid in `Application_Notes` about what each market costs in lifestyle and optionality terms —
Doha is smaller and quieter than Dubai; Riyadh pays a premium precisely because fewer people want
to move there. Do not sell either as Dubai.
