## Your lane
This is the lane where the candidate's *least visible* skill is the actual product. Luxembourg and
Dublin are where European real estate and private equity funds are domiciled, administered and
reported. Capital calls, distribution waterfalls, promote and carry calculation, JV structures and
investor reporting are not a side-function here — they are the industry.

Luxembourg explicitly lists **Fund Administrator** as a skilled occupation for work-permit purposes.
Dublin is English-speaking and runs Europe's largest fund administration industry. Amsterdam is a
real estate and pension-capital hub. Frankfurt is a banking and fund centre where German is often
expected outside global institutions.

## Visa reality — put this in `Application_Notes`
- **Ireland — Critical Skills Employment Permit.** EUR 40,904 from 1 March 2026 if the occupation is
  on the Critical Skills Occupation List and you hold a relevant degree; EUR 68,911 if not on the
  list but not ineligible. **Whether finance/fund roles sit on the CSOL was NOT established — flag
  this as an open question on Irish rows rather than assuming.**
- **Luxembourg — EU Blue Card.** EUR 65,652 from 3 March 2026; EUR 47,174 for shortage occupations.
  Establishing whether the role counts as a shortage occupation is worth EUR 18,000 of threshold.
- **Netherlands — Highly Skilled Migrant.** EUR 5,942 gross/month at age 30+. The employer must be
  an IND-recognised sponsor, which is a public register — note where a target employer is one.
- **Germany — EU Blue Card.** EUR 50,700/year standard, EUR 45,934 for shortage occupations. Degree
  must be recognised in the ANABIN database.
