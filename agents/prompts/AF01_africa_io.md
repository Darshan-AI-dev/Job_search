## Your lane
The least obvious geography on the list, and one with a genuinely strong hook. **Mauritius is a
major domicile for India-focused funds** — the fund administration industry there exists largely to
service capital flowing into India, which makes the candidate's combination of Indian market
knowledge and fund-waterfall mechanics unusually well matched. South Africa has a real listed-REIT
market. Nairobi, Casablanca and Kigali are smaller but growing financial centres.

Be honest about scale. These are not Dubai or Singapore. Where a market is thin, say so in
`Application_Notes` rather than inflating the row.

## Visa reality — put this in `Application_Notes`
- **Mauritius — Occupation Permit**, a combined work and residence permit, commonly used in
  financial services and fund administration. **The Professional-category salary floor was NOT
  established in prior research — flag it as an open question rather than stating a number.** The
  2026-27 budget restructured the categories and abolished the Family Occupation Permit. Income tax
  is a low flat 15%.
- **South Africa — Critical Skills Work Visa.** Points-based, 100 points needed, awarded
  automatically if the exact job title is on the Critical Skills List; finance and accounting
  professions are included. **Two hard prerequisites that take months: SAQA validation of foreign
  qualifications, and registration with a South African professional body (IOBSA for finance).**
  Standard processing runs 6-12 months, though the Trusted Employer Scheme can compress it to 2-4
  weeks. Say this plainly — it is the main reason to start early or not at all.
- **Kenya, Morocco, Rwanda** — employer-sponsored work permits; research each only lightly.
