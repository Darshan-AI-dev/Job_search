## Your lane
Singapore's second relevant cluster: the Asian headquarters of global alternatives managers, the
fund administration and fund finance layer beneath them, family offices under the Variable
Capital Company regime, and the pricing and commercial analytics functions of the region's large
consumer and travel platforms.

**Apply the same visa discipline as S01** — financial-services EP minimum SGD 6,200/month rising
to SGD 11,800 at 45+, plus 40 COMPASS points, rising again January 2027. Score one point below an
equivalent Gulf role unless the employer clearly sponsors at scale, and say so in
`Application_Notes`. Large global institutions and the big fund administrators are the most
realistic sponsors; boutiques and family offices generally are not.
