## Your lane
Australia runs one of the world's most mature listed-property and superannuation markets, and a
genuinely large build-to-rent sector that is professionalising fast — which is precisely the
candidate's discipline. New Zealand is a much smaller market and should be a minority of your rows;
do not pad it.

## Visa reality — put this in `Application_Notes`
- **Australia — Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482).** Two relevant streams. **Core Skills**:
  AUD 79,499/year from 1 July 2026, and the occupation must appear on the Core Skills Occupation
  List (456 occupations). **Specialist Skills**: AUD 146,717/year, with **no occupation-list
  requirement** — the salary is the filter. For a senior finance hire the Specialist Skills stream
  is often the cleaner route, and it is worth saying which stream a given row would realistically
  use.
- **New Zealand — Accredited Employer Work Visa.** The general median-wage requirement was removed
  in March 2025; median wage is NZD 35.00/hour from 9 March 2026 and still governs partner support
  and some settings. **The employer must already hold Immigration NZ accreditation** — that is a
  hard gate, so flag it where you can tell.
